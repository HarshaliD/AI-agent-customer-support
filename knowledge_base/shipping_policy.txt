NovaMart Shipping Policy

Standard shipping normally takes 3-5 business days after the order has been shipped.

Orders are normally processed before shipment within 1-2 business days.

Delivery times may be longer during holidays, major sales events, or unexpected carrier delays.

Customers can contact NovaMart support if an order has not arrived within the expected delivery period.

NovaMart support may request the customer's order number to investigate a delayed shipment.
