NovaMart Product FAQ

Q: How can I find my order number?
A: The order number can normally be found in the order confirmation email or in the customer's NovaMart account.

Q: Can I return an unused product?
A: Yes. An unused product can generally be returned within 30 calendar days of delivery, provided the other return requirements are satisfied.

Q: How long does a refund take?
A: For an approved return, the refund is normally processed within 5-7 business days after the returned product has been received and inspected.

Q: Does the warranty cover accidental damage?
A: Accidental damage is generally not covered by the manufacturer's warranty.

Q: How long does standard shipping take?
A: Standard shipping normally takes 3-5 business days after the order has been shipped.
