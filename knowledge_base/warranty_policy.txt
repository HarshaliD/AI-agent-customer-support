NovaMart Warranty Policy

NovaMart products are covered by the manufacturer's warranty when a warranty is provided for that product.

Warranty coverage depends on the individual product and manufacturer.

Warranty generally covers manufacturing defects that occur during the applicable warranty period.

Accidental damage, liquid damage, physical damage caused by misuse, and unauthorized modifications are generally not covered.

Customers should provide their order number and product information when requesting warranty assistance.

NovaMart support may request photographs or other information to determine the appropriate next step.
