NovaMart Refund Policy

Refunds are issued after an approved return has been received and inspected.

For approved returns, refunds are normally processed within 5-7 business days after inspection.

The refund is issued to the original payment method.

If the original payment method cannot receive the refund, NovaMart support will contact the customer to determine an appropriate alternative.

Refund requests for orders above ₹50,000 require additional approval from a NovaMart support manager.

A refund request does not guarantee that the requested amount will be approved.
