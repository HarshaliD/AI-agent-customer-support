NovaMart Return Policy

Customers may request a return within 30 calendar days of the product delivery date.

To be eligible for a return:
- The product must be unused.
- The product must be in its original condition.
- All original accessories and documentation should be included.
- The customer must provide the order number.

Products that are damaged because of customer misuse are not eligible for a standard return.

If a product arrives damaged or defective, the customer should contact NovaMart support within 7 days of delivery.

Once a returned product is received and inspected, NovaMart will determine whether the return is approved.

Approved returns are eligible for a refund according to the refund policy.
